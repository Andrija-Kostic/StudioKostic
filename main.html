FAQ

Common questions about my services

Project timelines vary based on complexity. A logo design typically takes 1-2 weeks, while complete brand identity packages may take 3-4 weeks. Rush services are available.
All projects include vector files (AI, EPS, SVG) and high-resolution raster files (PNG, JPG) suitable for both print and digital use. Additional formats available upon request.
Most packages include 3-5 rounds of revisions. My goal is to ensure you're 100% satisfied with the final result. Additional revisions can be arranged if needed.
If you're not satisfied with the initial concepts, a full refund is available within the first week. After approval of concepts, a 50% refund is available before final delivery.
I offer flexible pricing options and packages to fit various budgets. Contact me to discuss your specific needs and I'll create a custom solution that works for you.
